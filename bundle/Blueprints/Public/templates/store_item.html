Why We Built This

Every year, students struggle to manage their belongings during hostel transitions and semester breaks. Existing storage systems are manual, unreliable, and often lead to lost tokens and misplaced items.

TraceIt introduces a smarter approach by digitizing the entire process. From storing items to retrieving them, everything is tracked securely using digital tokens and structured storage management.

This ensures a seamless, transparent, and hassle-free experience for both students and administrators.

Digital QR Codes – Say goodbye to crumpled paper tokens! Get a digital QR code on your phone. Easy to save, impossible to lose, and always there when you need your stuff back.

Super Secure Storage – Your belongings are locked up tight in our monitored storage area. Plus, everything is digitally tracked so you know exactly where your stuff is, 24/7.

Quick answers

Smart Solutions for Lost and Found Items

No. TraceIt allows users to report lost or found items without creating an account. However, registering gives you the ability to track your reports and receive updates about possible matches.

TraceIt compares item details such as name, category, description, and location between lost and found reports. When a potential match is identified, the system alerts the user for verification.

If you find an item, you can report it through the “Report Found Item” section. Providing accurate details helps the system match the item with someone who has reported it as lost.

When a possible match is detected, TraceIt will notify you using the contact information you provided so you can verify the item and arrange its return.
